Price Intelligence Engine: Mytek × Tunisianet

Problem

Shoppers compare laptop prices by hand across sites, and the same laptop is named differently on each platform, so price differences are easy to miss.

Solution

A Python pipeline that scrapes both retailers, normalises titles and prices, matches products with RapidFuzz (85%+ similarity) and generates an Excel report with the cheapest vendor and the savings. It also documents product data that both sites expose in their frontend code.

Turkeydrives almost all of the oil effect on inflation among importers; for the others, exchange rates and policy credibility matter more

Econometrics, individual project

The Geopolitics of Oil: 2026 Gulf Crisis and Inflation

Question

How much did the oil price shock from the 2026 Gulf Crisis pass through to inflation, and does it hit every oil importer the same way?

Approach

A panel of 8 countries from January 2023 to April 2026 (320 monthly observations), modelled with pooled OLS, fixed and random effects in R. The Hausman test selects fixed effects, and results hold with robust standard errors.

In the repo

  • R notebook with the models and tests
  • Panel dataset built from IMF, OECD, FRED, BIS and U.S. EIA data
  • Research report

US Airline Twitter Sentiment Analysis

Problem

Airlines receive thousands of tweets a day and have no scalable way to process them, so useful customer feedback gets lost.

Solution

An NLP pipeline in Python that cleans the tweets, builds TF-IDF features (about 3,700 terms) and classifies sentiment with logistic regression, with K-Means clustering to test whether sentiment groups appear without labels. Customer service issues are the top reason for negative tweets (31.7%).
